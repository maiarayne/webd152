The Yurts at Pacific Trails Resort

What is a yurt?

Our luxury yurts are dome-shaped structures that offer a unique blend of traditional design and modern comfort.

How are the yurts finished?

Each yurt is finished with high-quality materials and modern amenities to ensure a comfortable and enjoyable stay.

What should I bring?

Most guests prefer to bring comfortable clothing, a light jacket, and any personal items they might need for their stay.
